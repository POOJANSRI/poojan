🏛️ About the Department

Shaping the Future of
Computer Science

The Applied Computer Science & Engineering (ACSE) Department at Vignan's Foundation is dedicated to academic excellence, technical skills development, and holistic student growth.

  • Undergraduate B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering
  • State-of-the-art computer labs and research facilities
  • Regular technical workshops, seminars, and guest lectures
  • Active student chapters: IEEE, CSI, and Coding Club
  • Competitive exam preparation and certification support
🚀 Student Activities

Discover Our Events

The ACSE department actively organizes a wide range of events to foster technical skills, leadership, and overall student development.

Hackathons & Ideathons

24-hour coding sprints and startup ideation events where students build innovative solutions for real-world problems.

Paper Presentations

Platform for students to present their research papers, technical articles, and innovative project ideas to industry experts.

Cultural Fests

Annual cultural extravaganzas featuring dance, music, arts, and drama, celebrating the diverse talents of our students.

Guest Lectures

Interactive seminars and workshops conducted by eminent industry professionals and alumni to bridge the academia-industry gap.

Sports Tournaments

Inter-department and university-level sports competitions to promote physical fitness, teamwork, and healthy competition.

Club Activities

Weekly meetups, coding challenges, and collaborative projects organized by student bodies like IEEE, CSI, and the Coding Club.
